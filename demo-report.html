B Overall Reviewer Assessment

The proposal addresses a relevant governance challenge in primary healthcare and has a promising practical direction. The next draft should focus on coherence across the case definition, conceptual framework and method. The comments below identify specific revisions for the student to address before proceeding to data collection.

C Priority Revisions

Priority 1 — Address Before Proceeding

Clarify the case structure and revise the conceptual framework so that the unit of analysis, possible embedded units and evidence sources are consistent.

Priority 2 — Important Improvements

Strengthen the rationale for sampling, triangulation and analytical steps; trace each research question to suitable evidence.

Priority 3 — Further Refinement

Tighten terminology, remove repeated background material and check referencing throughout.
